# Kodamaya (木霊屋) — game design

A cozy story + management game. Low-poly + pixel art, mixed 2D/3D, near-zero UI
(everything physical and in-world).

## Premise

**Mamoru** ("Mamo"), a 16-year-old Japanese pond turtle, has lost his dad. His
mom sends him to live with **Grandpa Genzō** in the faded shopping-street town
of **Kodama-machi**. Mamo is "hiding in his shell": hood up, earbuds in, always
on his phone. Grandpa runs **Kodamaya**, an old gachapon shop on **Kodama Ginza**
(a quiet, half-shuttered covered shotengai). The goal: make the street famous
and lively again, and let Mamo come out of his shell.

The shop secretly sells **Konyoki** (魂容器): soul containers made by spirits in
the **Spirit Realm**, reached through the shop's basement door. Each spirit makes
Konyoki that reflect itself.

## Cast

- **Mamoru** (player) — turtle, navy hoodie with a wave print, earbuds, phone.
- **Grandpa Genzō** — old turtle, indigo samue, round glasses, white brows and
  mustache, mossy shell, geta. Warm, a little sad, misses the Spirit Realm.
- **Bendo** — big silver fox, owner/chef of **Bendy's** (Wendy's parody: logo is
  a white-fox girl with red ribbon braids). Old friend of Grandpa.
- **Yuki** — Bendo's daughter, white fox, Bendy's waitress (romance).
- **Mio** — clumsy otter schoolgirl, sailor uniform (romance).
- **Kanako** — refined crab girl, kimono, shops at the thrift store; the
  hardest romance.
- Locals (animal-human, Japanese species): Shibata-san (shiba, breakfast
  kissaten "Morning Shibata"), Saru-san (macaque, café), Usami-san (hare,
  thrift store "Usagi Recycle"), Anaguma-jii (badger, sweet-potato cart
  "Yaki-imo~"), Ponta (tanuki fishmonger), Tsuru-baa (crane, sweeps the street),
  kids (tanuki, shiba, kitten, rabbit, macaque, fox, deer, bear cub) and parents.
- Train attendant (sika deer, wagon service).
- Spirits: **Tarōbō** the Tengu (red face, long nose, white mane, yamabushi
  robe with pompoms, crow wings, feather fan, rides a cloud; rich, classy,
  greedy, hot-tempered uncle; loves good tea and his pet crow **Kuro**, who
  keeps getting lost), **Kappei** the Kappa (green, beak, head dish, shell,
  cucumber floatie, hot spring + cucumber farm island; loves cucumber salad),
  **Hokori** (dust-bunny spirits with googly eyes and grey fuzz, summoned from
  collected dust; can fuse into a human shape, wear server/barista outfits,
  teleport with a dust poof, but carry goods physically), **Karakasa** (the
  umbrella yokai who makes flower umbrellas), the **six-legged sky cow**,
  the shop's **Maneki-neko** (decoration until awakened with Happiness; then
  it harvests Happiness automatically).

## Two worlds, one loop

- **Human World daytime**: run the shop (restock machines from the toy-box
  storage, exchange yen for gacha coins, greet and help customers, clean,
  decorate with posters and toys), collect yen and **Happiness**, explore the
  street, relationships, quests, build attractions.
- **Human World night = Spirit Realm daytime**: harvest, make tea, build,
  explore, deal with spirits, prepare Konyoki batches for tomorrow.

## Currencies & things

- **Yen**: from coin exchange. Renovate, buy floors, rent nearby shops, buy
  from **Cameri** (Mercari parody app on Mamo's phone: second-hand collectibles,
  furniture, tea equipment; sellers, conditions, rarity, price gaps, delivery
  wait or send a Hokori to pick it up).
- **Happiness**: colourful glowing star-candy (konpeito) blobs that pop out of
  happy kids. Swipe to collect; they fly into the **Happiness jar** (physical,
  fills visibly, has a capacity, upgradeable). Tea plants grow from Happiness;
  Tengu charges Happiness to clear clouds; spirits need it to make Konyoki.
- **Konyoki series** (gachapon): made in batches by spirits, take time to
  manifest, revealed in a hatching ceremony (batch, colour, variant, rarity,
  secret). Examples: Karakasa "Hanagasa" flower umbrellas (5 versions);
  Kappei "Kyūri Office" (cucumber office workers), "Kyūri Squishies",
  "Cucumber Warriors" (playable action figures, expensive); Tarōbō "Tengu
  Treasures" (fan / crow toy that plays his sound, poses, accessories);
  pop-culture parodies like **Pokébowl** (a poke-bowl pocket monster).
- **Tea**: real processes and tools (harvest, wither, steam, roll, dry, roast,
  grind, ferment, age, boil, cool, measure, whisk, brew). Matcha, sencha,
  hojicha, genmaicha, sky-cow milk tea... Some steps manual, some automated
  by Hokori baristas.
- **Knowledge Tree**: a physical peach tree in the Spirit Realm; each skill
  grows a branch.
- **Collection display**: themed shelves (forest, river, waterfall, museum, old
  house) with silhouettes for missing pieces and locked silhouettes for
  secrets.

## Opening (story)

1. Shinkansen (Kodama service, all stops): rice fields, towns, tunnels, city;
   station announcements; Mamo on his phone with sad music; the attendant brings
   a bento.
2. Busy station: Grandpa waits with a sign, hugs Mamo.
3. Top-down walk from the station exit down the shotengai to Kodamaya.
4. Grandpa rolls up the shutter; the shop is dusty and messy. Mamo's room
   upstairs is full of boxes: cleaning tutorial (tap to move, tap to clean,
   physical animations).
5. Dinner at Bendy's: Bendo and Grandpa catch up, Mamo feels left out; omurice.
6. Sleep. Next day: storage, restock, cashier, first customers (natural, lively
   tutorial).
7. After closing: the key with a spirit keychain, the basement door becomes a
   portal; Grandpa's old house in the Spirit Realm; cleaning; Hokori; Tarōbō
   arrives on a cloud, grumbles, blows away clouds revealing Kappa Island and
   his castle; Kappei; tea; the day ends with everyone on tatami drinking tea
   while the spirits manifest Konyoki for tomorrow.
